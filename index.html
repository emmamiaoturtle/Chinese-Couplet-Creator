🧧 Chinese Couplet Creator

Choose Chinese characters and create your own couplet!

1. How to Play

A Chinese couplet has two matching lines of poetry.

  1. Choose a theme and the number of characters.
  2. Tap a box in the first or second line.
  3. Choose a Chinese character from the word bank.
  4. Make both lines meaningful and balanced.
  5. Check your work and preview your artwork!

2. Create Your Couplet

Upper Couplet — 上联

Tap a box, then choose a character.

Lower Couplet — 下联

3. Choose Chinese Characters

Each character has its English meaning. Tap a character to fill the selected box.

Horizontal Scroll — 横批

Choose a short phrase for the top of your door.

4. Explain Your Couplet

Select a box to begin.
